Parts every area shares

Letter tiles and thumb tiles

A to D in a 44 tile, drawn the same in every state and on every surface. On the phone the tiles sit on the green field in the thumb band; on the host they start as four empty slots under the slide's name. Picked is a 3px ring plus the word, never an inverted letter.

Room count numeral

The check-in count, drawn one way everywhere it appears: 700, tabular, a fixed-width slot so 38 to 39 to 100 never shifts the label, and "checked in" on its baseline.

State lockup

A state word with its glyph in front at cap height, never a glyph in a circle above the words. The glyph takes the word's weight: a 24 stroke beside 400 and 500 words, 32 beside 700 words.

Count sentence and the heading line

One lead number in the card's hero slot, and the other counts written as one line under it. Above it, the heading carries one state word and one muted fact line. The hero slot keeps its size and place from open to ended. Replaces the stat tiles, the badge row next to the heading and the live dot.

Glyph set

One construction: a 256 grid, round caps and joins, no fills. Stroke 24 at text sizes, 32 at display sizes. One meaning per glyph.

2464NameMeans
checkDone: checked in, sent, right answer, already checked in
xNot accepted: refused code, wrong pick, hide
lockA professor's or admin's rule stops this: answers locked, teams final, permanent admin, not on the staff list, staff-only control
clockTime: late arrival, sign-in ending, code expiry
warnSomething didn't load or needs the presenter: errors, offline, presenter notes
cameraStart the camera
eyeShown to the room: shared with students, show check-in
