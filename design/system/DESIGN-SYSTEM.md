# Design

How every screen looks: the tokens, the surfaces, the kit, the layout and states for each area, and the rules each place the app shows up follows (student phone, professor laptop, staff laptop, projector, door iPad, iOS app). The look is the GDG on Campus RCC design system ([github.com/Sam-T-G/rcc-gdg](https://github.com/Sam-T-G/rcc-gdg), `docs/04-brand/design-system/`): green leads, Google Sans, depth from tone before shadow, and nothing moves unless its state changed. Read its `README.md`, `foundations.md`, `components.md`, `motion.md` and `bright-lines.md` before a design change; this file says how the app applies them.

This file replaces `docs/design.md` in phase 2. It was written in phase 1 from direction 1 ("tonal depth"), which the judges picked, plus the grafts they asked for from directions 2 and 3. Source tags: `SYN-n` is item n of `research/SYNTHESIS.md`; `R:dimension` is a dimension of `system/RUBRIC.md`; `graft:lens/dir` is a judge graft; `BL` is GDG `bright-lines.md`. Paths in this file are relative to the phase 1 folder `D` (the run's scratchpad `design/`) until phase 2 moves them.

Open `system/kit.html` (phase 2: **#/ui**) to see every part in every state; render it light and dark with `node D/tools/render.mjs --out <dir> system/kit.html`.

## How the stylesheets stack

`tokens.css` declares three cascade layers in order: `tokens`, `base` (`index.css`), `kit` (`kit.css`). Team stylesheets have no layer, so a team rule beats every base and kit rule, whatever its selector. Two things follow:

- A page can restyle a kit part with one plain class rule, no `!important` and no long selectors.
- A broad team rule such as `.host-root button { ... }` also restyles every kit button inside it. Scope team rules to the team's own classes. `contrast.test.ts` fails when an element rule under a page's `-root` class reaches a kit button or tab.

Phase 2 deletes the dead `host.css` rules (about 60 lines, INVENTORY "What each area's CSS adds"), the global `.row` and `button.quiet` overrides in `checkin.css`, and the per-team bar charts and big-number styles, since the kit now has one of each (R:craft).

Two base rules use `!important` on purpose, in a layer that beats every team rule: `[hidden]` always hides, and reduced motion zeroes spatial durations (effects stay, see "Motion").

Cascade layers need Safari 15.4, Chrome 99 or Firefox 97. Everything else in the kit works on iPadOS 16.2 (SYN-39); newer CSS sits in `@supports` with a fallback.

### Space, size and shape

- Space: `--s1` to `--s24` = 4px steps (4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 32, 40, 48, 64, 96). Nothing off the ladder (SYN-23).
- Page margin: phone 16 (8 around a field, 16 inside it), laptop 48 at 1366 and up, 32 from 1024 to 1365; kiosk 24; projector 96 stage px.
- Targets: phone and iPad 44 x 44 (`--tap`), answer tiles 72 tall 12 apart, kiosk buttons 56 to 72; laptop `.btn--sm` 32 with 8px gaps (R:fit).
- Laptop: bar 56, rows 44 (`--row-h`), right column `--aside-w` 400 (360 under 1366), content max 1344.
- Radius: GDG scale 4 / 8 / 12 / 16 / 24 / full, nested (inner = outer minus padding; SYN-9). Wells, fields, focal cards, sheets 24; cards and tiles 16; grouped rows 12 outer and 4 inner with 2px gaps; inputs 8; badges 4; buttons, tabs and switches full.

## Surfaces and elevation

Depth comes from tone: four rungs, one focal shadow, one overlay (SYN-1, SYN-2, BL 1.3 Depth). Shadows are tinted with `--ink`, two layers at most, never animated, never on the projector (SYN-3).

- One `.focal` per screen. If two things want it, the screen has two first things; pick one (R:hierarchy).
- The focal card is marked by its rung (raised fill plus `--shadow-1`) and by what it holds, never by a green rule. Round 1's 3px inset top rule drew as a crescent on the 24px radius and showed in every state, so it marked nothing (phone, host, staff, kiosk; LESSONS R1-01). If a screen needs a green cue on the focal card, it is the state's own badge (Open) beside the title.
- The loudest block below the focal card is never a well. In dark, a well that reads darker than a hole outranks the cards; keep `--well` one small step under the page (LESSONS R1-04).
- Every edge is a fill step or a deliberate ring (`--line-strong`, a state mark, the 3px select ring), never `--line` alone (SYN-5, R:depth 5). Round 2's staff sign-in card was white on the light page with only an `inset 1px --line` edge (1.44:1), the pale thin stroke Sam rejects: a standalone card on the page is the focal rung (`--hl`, `--shadow-1`), never a hairline card (staff-visual-2-3).
- In dark, the focal card must still read one rung up without the words. Dark `--shadow-1` is `inset 0 1px 0 rgb(255 255 255 / .06), 0 8px 24px rgb(0 0 0 / .45)`; round 2's host focal and side cards were both `#222823` and the old shadow barely showed (host-visual-2-7).
- Things on a field step up, never down, and stay in the field's hue. In dark a tile, pill or inset card on the green field is `--field-tile` `#1a6a35` with `--hl`; a fill equal to the page reads as a hole (R2-04) and a neutral grey reads as disabled (R3-07).
- **One raised object per screen; everything else sits flat** (round 4, LESSONS R4-02). Round 3's rule removed the shadow from secondary cards but builders kept them as white `--r-xl` cards, so every phone screen read as a pile of equal cards (slide card, leaderboard, checkbox row, folded slide row) and every host side block was the same raised card as the main one (phone-bespoke-4-1, phone-use-4-2, phone-use-4-10, host-bespoke-4-2, host-visual-4-9). ## Rules for each surface

### Student phone
iPhone Safari 390 x 844 and 320 wide, one hand, a glance between slides. Routes #/, #/checkin/student, #/checkin/join, #/live, #/portal.
- The state word or the question sits above the choices; choices full width in the thumb band, 72 tall, 12 apart (SYN-33).
- One primary per screen at `--lg`. No `.btn--sm`. Anything tapped is 44 x 44.
- Safe areas: `viewport-fit=cover` plus `env()` padding on the bar and the page bottom.
- Inputs 16px or more. No sideways scroll at 320.
- Loading, empty, error and signed-out states on every screen.

### Professor laptop (host)
1280 to 1440, at the lectern, read mid-sentence.
- The slide track across the top on every tab; the one live action in the focal card's header-row action slot (SYN-34).
- One filled green button. Row actions neutral ghosts on every row (round 4).
- Anything that can't be undone is a confirm; End day uses the overlay.
- Counts don't move when data lands: tabular numerals, fixed slots.

### Staff laptop
#/admin, #/checkin/teacher, #/checkin/quiz, attendance, #/lab, sign-in screens. Often in a hurry before class.
- The count line before the tables (no summary well since round 3); tables in a `--well` with a `--raised` head (round 4), rows 44, emails truncated, dates nowrap, numbers right.
- Remove and Unpair are ghost row actions on every row (no ring at rest) that open an in-row confirm.
- The page title sits in the bar; the first thing in the content is the count line or the task's focal card.
- Sign-in screens carry the logo and the independence sentence.

### Projector
#/checkin/stage, #/live/leaderboard, the late banner. Back row of A-210.
- Inside the 96 frame. Nothing under 32 stage px, sentences 40 or more, body 48. The one number 240; anything read alone 160.
- The QR is the same size in the same spot in every state; it never shrinks for a poll.
- No shadows, no hairlines: tone and 6px rules.
- Setup problems are presenter notes at 40 in the bottom-left lane. No emails, keys or addresses on the page.

### Door iPad (kiosk)
820 x 1180 portrait, about 1m, about 3 seconds.
- One layout: bar, 440 status panel, camera, typed fallback. Status word largest, name in a fixed slot.
- Every button 56 or more; Start check-in 72. Staff controls behind the 56 press-and-hold corner control. No back button for students.
- No blur, filter, loops or ambient motion. Text 16 or more.

## Where the app differs from GDG on purpose

- **Focus ring:** an outline (3px, 2px clear; 3px clear on kiosk and projector), where GDG uses a box-shadow, so a team rule can't hide it.
- **Disabled:** the button keeps its shape on the neutral fill (`--bar` light, `--overlay` dark) with `--muted` words and no ring (round 7, LESSONS R7-01), where GDG uses 38% opacity. Answer tiles never use opacity: unpicked tiles after sending take the solid `--tile-out` fill so a locked quiz answer stays readable (round 2).
- **Busy button:** the spinner sits over the words, so the width holds.
- **Line heights:** ratios, not px.
- **Ligatures:** off on everything.
- **Tap floor:** 44px for anything on a phone or iPad.
- **Dark rungs:** `--page` `#181c19`, `--raised` `#222823`, `--bar` `#2d332e`, `--overlay` `#383f39`, `--well` `#111512` are lifted from GDG's dark set so each rung above the page steps 1.15:1 or more and the well sits one small step (1.07) under the page. Dark `--accent-soft` `#26402c` is a green-tinted neutral, not GDG's container green, and dark `--field-tile` is `#1a6a35`, a step above the field in its own hue (round 3).
- **Live badge:** none on the web since round 3 (a state that never differs marks nothing; LESSONS R3-04). Open on host is a green state word. Amber stays late only (`research/round1-1.md`).
- **Dark field:** `#025620` (GDG green ramp tone 30), so white words on the field pass in dark.
- **Projector count:** 240 stage px and the 600 card are above GDG `presentation.md`'s Big number default for the back row.
- **Team colors:** twelve named team hexes, more than GDG's three-hue chart cap (BL 1.3 Charts). They are names, not a chart: always chip plus written word, a small square beside the name, never colored bars or a row tinted by team, and Red, Yellow, Blue and Green are shifted off the licensed four. A class with Red, Yellow, Blue and Green teams still puts all four on one leaderboard; that needs Sam's call (see Not done yet).
- **Leaderboard reorder:** instant with a held ring, per GDG motion 5.7 (no FLIP).
